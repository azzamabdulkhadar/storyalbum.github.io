# Aesthetic One-Liners

-   Tum meri favourite "what if" nahi... meri favourite "finally" ho.
-   Kuch log milte hain... aur phir ghar jaisa sukoon de jaate hain.
-   Mohabbat awaaz nahi karti... bas dil mein reh jaati hai.
-   Agar lafzon ki rooh hoti, toh woh tumhara naam leti.
-   Dil ki sabse khoobsurat jagah par tum rehte ho.
-   Tumhari yaad bhi tumhari tarah khoobsurat hai.
-   Har baar tumhe dekh kar lagta hai... duniya itni buri bhi nahi.
-   Tum meri dua ka woh hissa ho jo kabhi alfaaz nahi ban saka.
