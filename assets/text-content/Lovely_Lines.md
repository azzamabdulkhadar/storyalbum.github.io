# Lovely Lines

-   Kuch log zindagi mein aate nahi... bas dil mein utar jaate hain.
-   Tumhari hasi meri favourite notification hai.
-   Agar sukoon ka koi chehra hota, toh shayad tumhara hota.
-   Tum mere din ki sabse khoobsurat wajah ho.
-   Har kahani mein hero zaroori nahi hota... kabhi kabhi ek muskurahat
    hi kaafi hoti hai.
-   Dil ko ghar mil gaya jab se tum mile.
-   Tum sirf pasand nahi... aadat ban gaye ho.
-   Tumhari khamoshi bhi bohot kuch keh jaati hai.
