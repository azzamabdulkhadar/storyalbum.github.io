# Deep Love Shayari

1.  Ajeeb si aadat ho tum, Door bhi raho... Toh bhi sabse kareeb lagte
    ho.

2.  Raat bhar chand se baatein ki, Usne poocha kis ki yaad hai? Maine
    muskura kar sirf tumhara naam liya.

3.  Na chaand chahiye, Na sitare chahiye, Bas ek tum ho... Aur woh bhi
    hamesha ke liye.

4.  Tumhari aankhon mein jo sukoon dekha, Uske baad duniya ki har jagah
    bechain si lagi.

5.  Kaash waqt bhi tumhari tarah hota, Jitna guzarta... Utna hi
    khoobsurat lagta.
