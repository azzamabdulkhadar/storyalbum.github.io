# Romantic Shayari

1.  Kahein tum chand ho, ya meri duaon ka jawab, Har dafa tumhe dekhoon,
    dil kahe bas tum hi lajawab.

2.  Teri muskurahat ne ajeeb sa jaadu kar diya, Jo dil kabhi kisi ka na
    hua, woh tera ho gaya.

3.  Tum se milna sirf ek mulaqat nahi, Lagta hai jaise meri adhuri
    kahani ka aakhri safha mil gaya.

4.  Kahein wo raat jaisa manzar tha, Ya teri aankhon ka asar tha, Jo bhi
    tha... Us lamhe mein sirf tera hi zikr tha.

5.  Har dua mein tera naam chupaya hai, Log kehte hain mohabbat chhup
    nahi sakti, Maine muskura kar sirf tera khayal bataya hai.

6.  Teri baatein chai ki pehli sip jaisi hain, Har roz chahiye... Aur
    kabhi kam nahi hoti.

7.  Tumhare baad kisi aur ko dekha hi nahi, Na isliye ke koi khoobsurat
    nahi tha, Bas meri nazar wafadar thi.

8.  Mohabbat ka hisaab nahi hota, Bas ek naam hota hai... Aur mere liye
    woh tum ho.
9. Uski muskurahat mein kuch toh baat hai,
    warna yun hi koi dil ke itne paas nahi aata.
10. Kuch rishte khamoshi se bhi gehre hote hain,
    bin kahe sab kuch samajh jaana bhi ek ibaadat hai.
11. Chaand ko dekha tha raat bhar,
    phir yaad aaya... usse zyada khoobsurat toh koi aur hai.
12. Woh jab door jaati hai toh lagta hai,
    koi apna hissa saath le jaata hai.   
13. Kuch log yaadon mein nahi,
    dil ke kisi khoobsurat kone mein rehte hain.
14. Uski ek muskurahat kaafi hai,
    ek ordinary din ko yaadgaar banane ke liye.
15. Woh saamne ho toh lafz kam pad jaate hain,
    aur door ho toh khayal zyada aa jaate hain.
